Methodology

MAKE IT HARD TO DISMISS.

01

Named allegations must point to evidence. Court records, government investigations, regulatory filings and high-quality primary reporting outrank viral claims.

02

Historical claims stay historical. Current improvements are shown beside older abuses instead of freezing companies in the past.

03

Industry risk is not automatically brand guilt. A cobalt labor-risk finding does not prove that one current phone contains cobalt from that mine.

04

Unknown information stays UNKNOWN. Missing supply-chain data is part of the story, not permission to invent precision.

05

Photography used as atmosphere is labeled illustrative when it is not evidence from the exact case.
